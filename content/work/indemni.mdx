## Problem

Before Indemni, compliance was manual, reactive, and impossible to audit.
Dispatchers relied on tribal knowledge. Verification was a phone call, a photo on
a text thread, a gut feeling.

![Carrier verification details](/work/indemni/fig-carriers-1.png)

![Carrier risk patterns](/work/indemni/fig-carriers-2.png)

![Carrier evaluation and scoring](/work/indemni/fig-carriers-3.png)

## Solution

The hardest design problem wasn't the UI — it was behavioural. We gated rate con
and pickup details until verification is complete, so brokers enforce driver
adoption because it saves them money downstream. Compliance becomes
self-reinforcing.

![Rate confirmation, gated until verification](/work/indemni/fig-rate-con.png)

![Verification in the field](/work/indemni/fig-hand-phone.png)

![Driver mobile app](/work/indemni/fig-phones.png)

## Outcome

Compliance that compounds. Every verified load makes the next one faster to
clear and harder to fake — turning trust into a measurable, defensible asset.

![Driver verification and audit trail](/work/indemni/fig-driver.png)
