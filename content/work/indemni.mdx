## Problem

Before Indemni, compliance was reactive. Verification happened over phone calls.
Evidence was scattered across texts and email chains. And by the time a dispatcher
suspected something was wrong, the truck was already on the highway. The pain
points weren't abstract: they showed up every single day.

![Before and after the redesign](/work/indemni/fig-before-after.png)

## How we solved it

We approached this as two interconnected design problems: the broker who needs to
triage dozens of active loads at once, and the driver who needs to complete one
clear task right now. This constraint shaped our design direction. Indemni now
covers the full lifecycle of a shipment, from carrier vetting before assignment
to GPS-stamped delivery evidence at drop-off.

## Outcome

The result is a system where verification completion is non-optional by design,
evidence is timestamped and tamper-evident, and brokers never have to wonder
what's happening with a load.
