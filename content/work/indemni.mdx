## Problem

Cargo theft is usually discovered after the fact — once the freight is already
gone. Brokers were stitching together spreadsheets, email threads, and gut feel
to decide who to trust.

![Before and after the redesign](/work/screen-411.png)

## What we did

We designed the web and mobile experience end to end: carrier verification flows,
anomaly detection surfaced in-context, and a rate-confirmation workflow that keeps
a clear audit trail.

![Carrier verification screens](/work/card-132.png)

![Rate confirmation workflow](/work/monitor-screen.png)

## Solution

A unified platform that makes cargo theft preventable, not just reportable —
across desktop and a companion mobile app for teams in the field.

![Indemni mobile app](/work/screen-1468.png)
