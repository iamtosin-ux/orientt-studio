## Problem

Before Indemni, compliance was manual, reactive, and impossible to audit.
Dispatchers relied on tribal knowledge. Verification was a phone call, a photo on
a text thread, a gut feeling.

When a claim landed or cargo disappeared, brokers had nothing defensible to show —
no verified identity, no GPS-stamped evidence, no paper trail that would hold up.

![Carrier evaluation and risk scoring](/work/indemni/cs-carrier.png)

![Before and after the redesign](/work/indemni/cs-before-after.png)

## Solution

The hardest design problem wasn't the UI — it was behavioural. Drivers needed to
complete verification for the system to work, but they had no reason to unless the
experience was frictionless and the reward was immediate. We solved this with a
gate: rate con and pickup details are locked until verification is complete. The
broker enforces driver adoption because it saves them money downstream. Compliance
becomes self-reinforcing.
